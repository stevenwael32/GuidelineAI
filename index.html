THE PIPELINE

Four steps, in order, every time.

Every answer moves through the same evidence pipeline — nothing is generated without first being retrieved and evaluated.

01

Ask

You enter a clinical guideline question in plain language.

02

Retrieve

The system searches the indexed guideline using semantic embeddings.

03

Evaluate

Retrieved evidence is scored for relevance and checked against safety gates.

04

Answer or Refuse

The system answers when evidence is sufficient, and refuses openly when it isn't.

REFUSAL AS A FEATURE

Refusal isn't an error state. It's the product working correctly.

When retrieval is weak, when a question falls outside the indexed guideline's scope, or when a safety gate trips, GuidelineAI says so plainly instead of guessing. Every refusal explains what was searched and what to try next.
